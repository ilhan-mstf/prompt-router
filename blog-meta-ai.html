Meta AI Joins Prompt Router: Expanding to 12 Leading AI Models

Today, we are thrilled to announce that Meta AI is officially supported on Prompt Router as our 12th frontline AI provider. With this release, prompt engineers, researchers, and creators can now test their prompts across an even broader spectrum of frontier models with a single click.

Why Meta AI Matters

Meta has fundamentally reshaped the generative AI ecosystem through its open-weights Llama architectures. Its consumer platform, Meta AI, provides direct access to state-of-the-art conversational reasoning, coding assistance, and real-time web retrieval:

  • Llama Frontier Reasoning: Exceptional benchmark performance on complex multi-turn reasoning, synthetic coding, and structured output formatting.
  • Real-Time Web Intelligence: Native web grounding allows Meta AI to cross-verify recent events, documentation updates, and market trends.
  • Creative Fluency: Nuanced natural language generation that rivals or exceeds proprietary closed-source alternatives.

A Balanced 12-Model Canvas

Adding Meta AI also perfects the layout of our workspace. On desktop viewports, our provider grid now renders as a completely balanced 3-row × 4-column layout (12 models total), eliminating any blank grid slots:

How Routing to Meta AI Works

Like every provider on Prompt Router, integration with Meta AI is instantaneous and frictionless:

  • Direct URL Parameter Prefilling: When you click the Meta AI button (or hit ⌘Enter with Meta AI selected), Prompt Router encodes your prompt into a clean query parameter (https://www.meta.ai/?q={encoded_prompt}).
  • No Extensions or Logins Required: You don't need to install any browser extensions, configure API keys, or create a Prompt Router account. Everything executes directly in your browser.
  • Instant Cross-Comparison: Test prompts from any of our 11 curated Prompt Libraries against Meta AI and immediately contrast the results with Claude or ChatGPT.
“Prompt comparison is the cornerstone of prompt engineering. By bringing Meta AI into the mix alongside OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and open-weights leaders like DeepSeek and Qwen, you get an instant 360-degree evaluation of your ideas.”

Completely Local-First & Private

Our commitment to privacy remains unwavering. Prompt Router has no backend database, tracks zero user prompts, and uses no analytics trackers. Your prompt text is strictly preserved in your local browser session and only sent to the AI providers you intentionally choose to open.
